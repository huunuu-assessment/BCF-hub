Welcome to your facilitator hub

A note from huunuu's founder, Clare

Welcome… and thank you.

What you're doing matters more than you might realise. Every time you create a space for someone to have a big conversation about life, love, loss or legacy, you're giving them something truly precious: permission to speak honestly and be heard.

These conversations aren't always easy, but they are always worth it. You're helping people connect more deeply with themselves and the people they love most, and that ripple effect reaches far beyond the room.

The hub is here to support you with everything you need, but the most important thing you bring is already yours: your courage, your compassion and your belief that talking about the big stuff makes life richer for all of us.

We're so glad you're here. Now let's get people talking.
